## 2026-11-06, CI on Ganymede 0.6.0 (#328)

Ganymede v0.6.0 is out, so CI installs 0.6.0 for e2e and the Mac app links
ganymede-ffi from the `v0.6.0` tag instead of a `main` commit. The tag
contains ganymede#390 (the ffi panic callback the crash reports need), which
was why `GANYMEDE_REF` pointed at `main`. Pinning to a tag again means the
app in the next ganymede-ui release is built from a released core.
