## 2026-11-01, Vite 8 (#147)

Vite 8.3.5 with `@vitejs/plugin-react` 6.1.2, both pinned exactly. Vite 8 bundles with Rolldown instead of Rollup and esbuild.

Notes:

- The ERESOLVE in the issue didn't come back: `npx npm@11 install` and `npm ci` resolve the tree without `--legacy-peer-deps`. `@rolldown/plugin-babel` is an optional peer of plugin-react 6 and nothing here installs it, so its Babel 8 chain isn't pulled in.
- plugin-react 5 stopped setting `resolve.dedupe: ['react', 'react-dom']`. JupyterLab's packages bring nested React 18 copies, and without the dedupe the find bar and cell toolbar tests failed with hooks reading a null dispatcher (two Reacts). `vite.config.ts` and `vitest.config.ts` now set it themselves.
- The `@jupyter-widgets/controls` package-version inliner is gone. Rolldown bundles the `require('../package.json')` inside that ES module, in the build and in dev pre-bundling, which esbuild and Rollup didn't (checked: `vite build` and `vite --force` both give `version` 5.0.13). `build.rollupOptions` became `build.rolldownOptions`, here and in the agent spike's config.
- New build-time packages: `rolldown` and its native bindings (MIT), `@oxc-project/types` (MIT), `lightningcss` and its bindings (MPL-2.0, Vite's CSS minifier), `detect-libc` (Apache-2.0). None reaches the built app. `react-refresh` and two Babel JSX plugins left.
- Dependabot groups `vite` with `@vitejs/*`, since plugin-react's majors follow Vite's.
