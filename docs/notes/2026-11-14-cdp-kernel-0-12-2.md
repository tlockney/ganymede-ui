## 2026-11-14, cdp-kernel 0.12.2 (#618)

cdp-kernel 0.12.2 is a docs release: its site shows the real output of
the `jupyter` examples and live widgets instead of screenshots. The
typings change only in comments, so nothing in the apps changes. The
dependency and CI's `CDP_KERNEL_VERSION` move to 0.12.2.
