## 2026-11-24, V8 and the engine benchmark leave the apps (#765)

The ganymede#646 decision kept the in-app TypeScript kernel on WebKit's
Worker, and Ganymede v0.16.0 dropped the `v8-bench` feature from
ganymede-ffi (ganymede#921). Pinning v0.16.0 in `GANYMEDE_REF` (the
v0.16.0 release, #784) broke the iOS test build:
`ganymede_v8_bench_available` no longer exists. #765 had been closed by
the docs-only #781 before this half was done; it is reopened, and this
change closes it. `GANYMEDE_REF` moves to v0.16.0 here too: v0.15.0's
XCFramework links V8, which needs `-lc++` and the stubs this removes.

Removed: Settings > Developer's engine benchmark (`EngineBenchmark`,
`EngineBenchmarkReport`, `BenchWorkerPage`, their tests,
`apple/Resources/Bench`, `update-bench.sh`, `bench-licenses.mjs` and the
bundle check), `Sources/V8iOS/WasmStubs.c`, `-lc++` on the app and test
targets, and the extended virtual addressing entitlement on Ganymede-iOS
with release-ios.sh's profile check. `PreviewResources` goes back to the
Quick Look preview's scheme and host only, and out of the app targets.
Settings > Developer keeps the crash report tests; General's toggle
footer says so. docs/apps.md, docs/releasing.md and apple/README.md
follow. The App ID's capability can stay on: a profile that includes it
signs an app that doesn't ask for it.

Tests: CI's macos-app and ios-app builds and tests (no Swift toolchain
here). The iOS build failure on #784 is the check this fixes. The app
size drop (about 19 MB of iOS binary) is a by-hand check in #273, on
the first TestFlight build with this change.
