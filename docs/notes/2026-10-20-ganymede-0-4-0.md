## 2026-10-20, Ganymede 0.4.0 and cdp-kernel 0.7.1 in CI (#108)

- The e2e job installs Ganymede 0.4.0, the release that ships
  `ganymede ui` with this app's 0.2.0 as its default. cdp-kernel 0.7.1
  came in with #110.
- The macOS app job builds ganymede-ffi from the `v0.4.0` tag instead of
  an untagged `main` commit: 0.4.0 is the first release with
  ganymede-ffi and the gateway.
