## 2026-10-18, served by Ganymede in e2e (#36)

Ganymede 0.3.0 sets the `_xsrf` cookie on pages from a plain
`--static-dir`, so the e2e job now pins 0.3.0 and its `ganymede serve`
also serves `dist/`. `e2e/served.spec.ts` opens the token URL on
Ganymede's own port: the server trades the token for a login cookie and
redirects, the app connects on the cookie without showing the sheet, runs
a cell and saves through the `_xsrf` cookie. It works on a copy of the
fixture so the other specs keep the notebook as committed.
